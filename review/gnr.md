# Good News Reader — Traditional edition (source text)

福音讀本 (Good News Reader, AFC / Happy Hour Publishing). The published book is
**Simplified**; this file is the **Traditional** conversion, transcribed from the
scanned PDFs and kept as we go so a full **Traditional translated edition** can be
assembled later. This is the human-readable companion to `content/gnr-chapter-01.js`
(the app's data form). Canonical script = Traditional; the app's picker renders
Simplified again at display time.

Transcribed so far: **Lessons 1–3.** (Lesson 0 "Introduction to Radicals" is a
reference table, not yet included; lessons 4–25 + appendices + glossary pending.)

## Lesson 1 — The Existence of God  第一課　上帝的存在

### Chinese (Traditional)

上帝是存在的。他真的存在。在中國，有很多人說：「沒有上帝！」這是不對的。你信上帝，他存在，你不信他，他也存在！

不信上帝的人，心裡很空虛，因為他們不知道他們為什麼而生、為什麼而死；他們不知道上帝真的存在。上帝是愛他們的。

### English

God exists. He really exists. In China many people say, "There is no God!" This is not right. If you believe in God, He exists; if you do not believe in Him, He still exists!

People who do not believe in God are very empty in their hearts, because they do not know why they were born or why they die; they do not know that God really exists. God loves them.

## Lesson 2 — The Creator of the Universe  第二課　宇宙的創造者

### Chinese (Traditional)

上帝是存在的，但是我們看不見他。為什麼我們看不見他呢？因為他不是宇宙的一部分；他是宇宙的創造者。

上帝創造了整個宇宙，大到星系，小到原子，都是他創造的。上帝也創造了你和我；他愛我們。這是真的！

### English

God exists, but we cannot see Him. Why can't we see Him? Because He is not a part of the universe; He is the Creator of the universe.

God created the whole universe — from galaxies as large as they are to atoms as small as they are, all of it He created. God also created you and me; He loves us. This is true!

## Lesson 3 — God Is Infinitely Great  第三課　上帝無限偉大

### Chinese (Traditional)

宇宙只有一位創造者，只有一位上帝。這位上帝是非常偉大的！世界上的東西都在改變，只有上帝永遠不改變。

世界上的東西都是有限的，只有上帝是無限的。上帝的力量是無限的，他的智慧是無限的，他的愛也是無限的。上帝真是太偉大了！

### English

The universe has only one Creator, only one God. This God is extremely great! Everything in the world is changing; only God never changes.

Everything in the world is limited; only God is infinite. God's power is infinite, His wisdom is infinite, and His love is also infinite. God really is too great!
